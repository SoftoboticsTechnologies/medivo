Medical Imaging Equipment Installation

Mechanical installation covers the physical side of bringing an imaging system into service: moving it into the room, setting it on its final position, assembling components and completing the mechanical fixings the equipment requires.

The work is sequenced with the equipment supplier and the facility so that room readiness, access routes and installation windows line up rather than conflict.

Site Coordination

Imaging rooms are rarely empty sites. Access routes pass through working departments, other trades are often on site, and the room itself may still be under construction.

Coordination therefore forms part of the scope: agreeing routes and timings, confirming what has to be protected or temporarily removed, and keeping the facility informed at each stage.
