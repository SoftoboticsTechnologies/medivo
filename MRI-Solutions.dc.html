What We Provide

Medivo Healthcare supplies specialized products and accessories used inside and around the MRI environment, including MRI patient trolleys, MRI wheelchairs, MRI oxygen cylinders, MRI pulse oximeters and MRI-compatible patient monitoring equipment.

The range also covers patient-care accessories, room accessories, patient transfer and positioning equipment, and safety accessories used across MRI suites.

Built for the MRI Environment

Products intended for use in MRI environments are selected based on their applicable safety classification, intended use and manufacturer specifications, since equipment brought near the magnet has to be appropriate for that setting.

The portfolio is continuously expanded through partnerships with qualified manufacturers and suppliers, so facilities have a single point of contact for MRI patient-care and room accessories.
