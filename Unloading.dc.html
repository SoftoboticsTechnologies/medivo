Heavy Equipment Handling

Imaging systems arrive as single heavy units on transport that cannot reach the room. Unloading is the stage where the equipment leaves the vehicle and enters the building, and it is the point where most handling risk sits.

Lifting arrangements, ground conditions, load spread and the movement route are worked out before the vehicle arrives, so the sequence on the day is planned rather than improvised.

Site Access Planning

Access is assessed in advance: approach and standing position for the vehicle and crane, door and corridor dimensions, floor loading, level changes, and anything that has to be temporarily removed.

Where access is restricted, the route is agreed with the facility ahead of delivery along with timing that keeps disruption to departments to a minimum.
