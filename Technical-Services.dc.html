Beyond Product Supply

Medivo Healthcare provides more than product supply. Professional technical and project-support services cover installation, unloading, handling, shifting, relocation and dismantling of medical equipment.

Work is coordinated according to project requirements and applicable OEM and site specifications, with appropriate equipment, planning and safety procedures at every stage.

Site & Project Coordination

Healthcare projects involve multiple stakeholders. Technical and project coordination is provided for medical equipment installations, working closely with hospitals, diagnostic centres, OEMs, contractors and other stakeholders.

Additional equipment-related technical and project-support services are available based on customer and project requirements.
