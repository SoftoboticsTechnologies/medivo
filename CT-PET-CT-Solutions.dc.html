Supporting CT & PET-CT Workflows

Medivo Healthcare provides equipment, accessories and consumables that support CT and PET-CT imaging workflows, including CT injectors, PET-CT injectors and the injector consumables that keep a contrast-media workflow running day to day.

The range extends to syringes, patient sets and contrast-media administration accessories, so departments can source the recurring consumables and the accessories around them from one supplier.

Quality-Focused Sourcing

CT and PET-CT departments depend on injector consumables being available and consistent, since a workflow interruption affects patient throughput. Products are sourced with that reliability in mind.

The portfolio continues to expand through partnerships with qualified manufacturers, suppliers and healthcare technology companies working in CT and PET-CT imaging.
