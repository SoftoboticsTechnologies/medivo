About Medivo Healthcare Private Limited

Who We Are

Medivo Healthcare Private Limited is an emerging healthcare solutions company specializing in medical equipment, imaging accessories, consumables and professional technical services for hospitals, diagnostic centres, imaging centres, oncology facilities, Cath-Labs and other healthcare institutions.

A company built around imaging & healthcare equipment

Medivo Healthcare is focused on supporting critical healthcare environments including MRI, CT, PET-CT, Cath-Lab, Medical Imaging and Oncology, with a comprehensive range of products and services designed to meet the evolving requirements of modern healthcare facilities.

Our portfolio includes MRI patient-care equipment and accessories, CT and PET-CT accessories, injectors and consumables, Cath-Lab accessories, medical-grade monitors and other specialized medical equipment. In addition to product supply, we provide professional technical and project-support services — installation, unloading, handling, shifting, relocation and dismantling — for hospitals, diagnostic centres, imaging centres and other healthcare institutions.
