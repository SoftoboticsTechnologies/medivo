Technical Expertise Behind Medical Imaging Infrastructure

Medivo Healthcare works in medical imaging infrastructure, covering RF shielding, quench pipe systems, and mechanical installation for hospitals, diagnostic centres and research facilities.

Who We Are

A company built around imaging infrastructure

Medivo Healthcare provides technical services for medical imaging infrastructure. The work covers RF shielding for MRI rooms, quench pipe systems, mechanical installation and dismantling of imaging equipment, unloading of imaging systems on site, and passive shielding solutions.

Projects are delivered for hospitals, diagnostic centres, imaging centres, research facilities and medical equipment providers, and each scope is planned around the equipment involved and the conditions of the site.
